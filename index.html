10. Data and methodology
Salt-front definition: the most inland monitored station where observed salinity is at or below the selected threshold, provided station distances are known and the observations are temporally comparable.

Online hydrology: modelled daily river discharge and rainfall. These variables provide hydrological context but are not direct salinity observations.

Forecasting: the online discharge outlook is not a trained salinity forecast. A calibrated salinity model requires observed salinity training data and independent validation.
Data attribution: Department of Water Resources, The Gambia; Open-Meteo; Copernicus Emergency Management Service (GloFAS).
