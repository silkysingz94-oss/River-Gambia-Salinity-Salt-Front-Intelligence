05. Data Sources & Methodology

TRANSPARENCY
Official and supporting sources
  • DWR — Official station directory
  • DWR — Hydrological services
  • DWR — Data request portal
  • Afrimet — Gambia hydrology station information
  • Open-Meteo — Flood API documentation
  • Copernicus — GloFAS historical discharge
DWR states that its hydrological services collect river and groundwater levels and physicochemical measurements including pH, salinity and temperature. This dashboard does not assume those observations are publicly downloadable through the station directory.
Methodology
  1. Read the observation CSV and validate the required date, station and salinity_psu fields.
  2. Apply the selected station and date filters before calculating statistics.
  3. Calculate station-level and monthly salinity summaries from the loaded observations.
  4. Compare readings against the selected freshwater threshold.
  5. Estimate a station-based freshwater boundary only where station locations, river distances and observation dates support the comparison.
Modelled river discharge, rainfall and weather variables are not observed salinity. A salinity forecast requires suitable historical salinity observations for model training and independent validation.
